Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking, setup, staffing and customization.

1. What types of carnival games do you provide?

We provide classic carnival games, skill games, family attractions and themed entertainment booth concepts for a wide range of events.

2. Are your games suitable for children and adults?

Yes. Our setup is designed for family entertainment, with options suitable for children, teens and adults depending on the event type.

3. Do you provide staff to manage the booths?

Yes. Staff support can be arranged for setup, supervision and guest interaction based on your event requirements.

4. Can the booths be customized for a brand or themed event?

Yes. We can tailor game selection, colors, presentation and decorative elements to better suit your activation or celebration theme.

5. How much space do you need for setup?

The required space depends on the number of booths and attractions selected. We can recommend a suitable layout after reviewing your venue.

6. How early should we book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for seasonal festivals, school events and large public activations.

7. Can you set up indoors and outdoors?

Yes. Our carnival concepts can work for indoor malls, outdoor plazas, parks, compounds and private venues, subject to event conditions.

8. What is included in the setup package?

Packages may include booth structures, game props, prize displays, decorative lighting and staffing support, depending on the selected package.

9. Do you provide prizes and giveaways?

Yes. Prize displays and giveaway concepts can be included, and they can be adjusted according to the event budget and audience.

10. How can we get a quote?

Send us your event date, location, audience size and the type of activities you need. We can then prepare a tailored quotation.
