About Premier Projects

Building Partnerships. Delivering Quality. Creating Value.

Established in 2026, Premier Projects for Food Trading and Services is a diversified trading and services company established as a sister company of Premier Catering Services.

Under the leadership of Mr. Abdulla Saoud O H Al Mana, the group has expanded beyond catering into multiple commercial sectors.

Vision & Mission

To become a trusted diversified trading and services company in Qatar and the region, recognized for quality products, reliable services, strong partnerships and responsible business practices.

We provide reliable products, competitive pricing and professional services while developing dependable international supply networks.
