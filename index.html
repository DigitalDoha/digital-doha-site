About Premier Projects

Building Partnerships. Delivering Quality. Creating Value.

Established in 2026, Premier Projects for Food Trading and Services is a diversified trading and services company established as a sister company of Premier Catering Services.

Under the leadership and supervision of SALMEEN ABDULRASHEED MA E.AHMEDNOOH, the group has expanded beyond catering into multiple commercial sectors serving Qatar and international markets.

Our Foundation

We bring together experience, market knowledge and a strong commitment to quality, reliability and professional service. Our objective is to build long-term relationships with customers, suppliers, contractors and business partners.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and diversified trading and services company in Qatar and the region, recognized for quality products, reliable services, strong partnerships and responsible business practices.

Our Mission

To provide reliable products, competitive pricing and professional services across multiple business sectors while developing dependable international supply networks and long-term customer relationships.
